Fabian Nøst Harang

Professor of Data Science · BI Norwegian Business School

I am an applied mathematician working on stochastic analysis and the mathematics of data science, with interests in rough paths, stochastic differential equations, and signature methods for paths and time series.

I lead AMOR, the Center for Applied Mathematics and Operations Research at BI. I am also an Adjunct Chief Research Scientist and Director of Research, SURE-AI, at Simula.

Research

My work connects pathwise methods and stochastic modelling with artificial intelligence and data science, particularly learning from complex time series. Industry applications focus on forecasting and decision-making under uncertainty in retail, transportation and finance.
